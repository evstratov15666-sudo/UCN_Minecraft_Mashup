# Ultimate Minecraft Night - Gameplay Guide

## Quick Start (2 minutes)

1. **Launch the game** → Office desk appears
2. **Select difficulty** → Adjust sliders for each enemy (0-20)
3. **Choose mode** → Single Player or Multiplayer
4. **Start night** → Survive until 6 AM countdown reaches 00:00

## Game Objectives

### Primary Goal
**Survive all 7 nights** by defending your office against:
- 22 animatronics (Ultimate Custom Night)
- 5 Minecraft creatures (Creepers, Endermen, Withers, Zombies, Skeletons)

### Secondary Goals
- Maximize your score
- Collect rare resources
- Complete crafting recipes
- Destroy maximum threats

## Resources & Inventory

### Starting Resources (Night 1)
- Stone: 10 blocks
- Dirt: 20 blocks
- Oak Planks: 8 blocks
- Sticks: 10 blocks

### Resources Increase Each Night
```
Night 1: 10 stone, 20 dirt, 8 planks, 10 sticks
Night 2: +5 stone, +5 dirt, +4 planks, +5 sticks, 5 sand
Night 3: +5 stone, +5 dirt, +4 planks, +5 sticks, +5 sand, 5 logs
Night 4: +5 stone, +5 dirt, +4 planks, +5 sticks, +5 sand, +3 logs, 3 iron ore
Night 5: +5 stone, +5 dirt, +4 planks, +5 sticks, +5 sand, +2 logs, +2 iron ore, 3 gunpowder
Night 6: +5 stone, +5 dirt, +4 planks, +5 sticks, +5 sand, +2 logs, +3 iron ore, +2 gunpowder, 1 diamond
Night 7: +5 stone, +5 dirt, +4 planks, +5 sticks, +5 sand, +3 logs, +2 iron ore, +3 gunpowder, +1 diamond, 5 iron ingots
```

## Combat Mechanics

### Block Placement
- **Cost:** 1 block per placement
- **Use:** Build walls, stairs, defensive structures
- **Protected Areas:** Cannot place on office desk, corridors, vents
- **Score:** +10 points per block

### Block Destruction
- **Method:** Click/attack the block
- **Time:** Varies by block type
- **Score:** +5 points per block
- **Protected Areas:** Cannot destroy critical infrastructure

### Dynamite (TNT)
- **Crafting:** 5 gunpowder + 4 sand
- **Effect:** Destroys blocks in 5-block radius
- **Damage:** Damages nearby mobs and destroyers
- **Score:** +50 points per use
- **Warning:** May destroy your own structures!

## Enemy Mechanics

### Animatronics
**Behavior:** Roam office, attempt to reach you
**Attack:** Jumpscare when they reach you
**Defense:** Block their paths with blocks

**Notable Animatronics:**
- Freddy Fazbear: Leads attacks
- Foxy: Fast, aggressive
- Puppet: Teleports
- Golden Freddy: Instant jumpscare if ignored
- Nightmare Animatronics: Highly aggressive (late nights)

### Minecraft Mobs

#### Creeper 💣
- **Behavior:** Approaches silently, explodes
- **Damage:** Destroys surrounding blocks
- **Defense:** Block it, use TNT to detonate from distance
- **Difficulty:** Medium

#### Enderman 👁️
- **Behavior:** Teleports around office, steals blocks from inventory
- **Damage:** Stealing your resources
- **Defense:** Keep it away from your inventory
- **Difficulty:** Hard

#### Wither ☠️
- **Behavior:** Boss mob, shoots projectiles, damages everything
- **Health:** High (300 HP)
- **Damage:** Massive, sustained attack
- **Defense:** Build reinforced shelter, use multiple TNT
- **Difficulty:** Very Hard

#### Zombie 🧟
- **Behavior:** Slow but persistent, melee attacks
- **Damage:** Direct attacks
- **Defense:** Block its path or use TNT
- **Difficulty:** Easy

#### Skeleton 🏹
- **Behavior:** Ranged attacker, shoots arrows
- **Damage:** Arrow projectiles
- **Defense:** Build cover, use TNT from distance
- **Difficulty:** Medium

## Difficulty System

### Adjusting Enemy Difficulty
- **Range:** 0-20 for each individual enemy
- **0:** Enemy inactive (ignored)
- **1-5:** Easy - rare appearances
- **6-10:** Normal - occasional attacks
- **11-15:** Hard - frequent attacks
- **16-20:** Extreme - constant threat

### Recommended Configurations

#### Easy Mode (Beginners)
- All Animatronics: 0-5
- All Mobs: 0-3
- **Focus:** Learn mechanics, build basics

#### Normal Mode (Casual)
- Animatronics: 5-10
- Mobs: 3-7
- **Focus:** Balanced challenge and fun

#### Hard Mode (Experienced)
- Animatronics: 10-15
- Mobs: 7-12
- **Focus:** Strategic gameplay required

#### Extreme Mode (Hardcore)
- Animatronics: 20
- Mobs: 20
- **Focus:** Pure survival challenge

## Multiplayer (Co-op)

### 2-4 Player Cooperation

**Setup:**
1. Host selects "Multiplayer" mode
2. Host configures difficulty
3. Host starts night
4. Join link is generated
5. Other players click link to join

**Shared Resources:**
- One collective inventory
- All players see same threats
- Coordinate defense strategies

**Win Condition:**
- All players survive until 6 AM
- If one dies, all fail the night

**Strategy Tips:**
- Divide roles: Crafter, Builder, Defender
- Assign block placement areas
- Concentrate TNT for boss fights
- Communicate threats via chat

## Scoring System

|--------|--------|

- Score is calculated per night

- Compare with other players (future update)

## Tips & Tricks

### Resource Management
- Don't waste blocks early - you'll need more later
- Prioritize crafting essential tools
- Save TNT for boss fights

### Defensive Strategies
- Build choke points to funnel enemies
- Create safe room behind locked doors
- Use elevation advantage (stairs/platforms)
- Place blocks around your desk

### Special Strategies

**Anti-Creeper:**
- Surround with water (explodes but blocks stay)
- Use TNT to trigger distant explosion
- Place doors to slow approach

**Anti-Enderman:**
- Store valuable items in chests
- Create enclosed safe zone
- Limit teleport paths

**Anti-Wither:**
- Build thick walls (needs 3+ layers)
- Use multiple TNT simultaneously
- Retreat and heal strategy

**Anti-Zombie:**
- Slow melee = time to prepare
- Create elevated platforms
- Use stairs as defense

**Anti-Skeleton:**
- Advance using building as cover
- Close distance quickly
- TNT from behind

## Night Progression

### Night 1 (Easy Warmup)
- Few enemies active
- Learn basic mechanics
- Establish base defense

### Night 2 (Difficulty Increases)
- More enemies appear
- Resource scarcity starts
- Crafting becomes essential

### Night 3-5 (Peak Challenge)
- Multiple simultaneous threats
- Strategic planning required
- Resource management critical

### Night 6-7 (Extreme)
- Highest difficulty available
- Boss enemies appear frequently
- Desperation tactics needed

## Victory Conditions

**Single Player:**
1. Survive all 7 nights
2. Reach final screen with victory message
3. Final score displayed

**Multiplayer:**
1. All players survive Night 7
2. Reach victory screen together
3. Combined/individual scores shown

## Game Over Conditions

You lose if:
- You take too much damage (animatronics reach you)
- Office is completely breached
- Time runs out (but this is victory!)
- You disconnect from session (multiplayer)
