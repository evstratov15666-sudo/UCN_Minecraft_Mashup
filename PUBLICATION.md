
### Слоган
```
Survive 7 nights against animatronics and Minecraft mobs.
Collect blocks, craft tools, defend your office.
```

### Описание (полное)
```
Ultimate Minecraft Night combines two iconic games into one intense experience.

You are trapped in an office. Every night, animatronics from Ultimate Custom Night 
and creatures from Minecraft attempt to reach you. Your only defense is your wit, 
your inventory of blocks, and your ability to craft.

**FEATURES:**
• 7 nights of increasing difficulty
• 22 unique animatronics + 5 Minecraft mobs
• Adjustable difficulty for each enemy (0-20)
• Real Minecraft crafting system
• Block placement and destruction mechanics
• Dynamite explosives for desperate situations
• Single player and co-op multiplayer
• Protected office locations

**GAMEPLAY:**
1. Sit at your office desk and configure the night
2. Select which enemies to face and their aggression level
3. Collect Minecraft blocks scattered throughout the office
4. Craft tools and defensive structures
5. Use dynamite to destroy threats
6. Survive until 6 AM to advance to the next night

This is a fan-made mashup created with Melty.gg. 
Requires ownership of both Ultimate Custom Night and Minecraft: Java Edition.
```

## Ошибки установки и их решения

### Проблема: "Games not found"
**Решение:** Убедитесь, что установлены обе игры:
- Ultimate Custom Night (Steam ID: 871720)
- Minecraft: Java Edition (Launcher)

### Проблема: "Offline mode required for LAN multiplayer"
**Решение:** Проверьте подключение к локальной сети

### Проблема: "UI не загружается"
**Решение:** Очистите кэш браузера и перезагрузитесь

## Публикация

1. **Создать GitHub репозиторий** (опционально)
   ```
   https://github.com/melty-gg/ultimate-minecraft-night
   ```

2. **Загрузить на Melty.gg**
   - Перейти в https://melty.gg/create
   - Выбрать "Upload Mashup"
   - Загрузить `dist/ultimate-minecraft-night-v0.1.0.tar.gz`
   - Заполнить информацию выше
   - Загрузить скриншоты
   - Submit для модерации

3. **Ожидание модерации** (обычно 24-48 часов)

4. **Публикация!**
   - Mashup появится в списке
   - Пользователи смогут его устанавливать
